About the app

About AI Voice

A browser-based text-to-speech app that uses voices available on your device.

Browser Speech

Speak entered text using the actual voice names and language codes supplied by your browser and operating system. Adjust rate, pitch, and volume, then play, pause, resume, or stop speech.

Available voices

Voice choices vary by browser, operating system, and installed system voices. The app lists available voices directly rather than inventing options.

Long text

Long text is split into ordered chunks for playback. You can stop speech at any time.

Built with the Web Speech API.
